Build your first model

  1. Add a piece. Choose from solid shapes and useful parts. New pieces sit beside your design.
  2. Change its size. Select it and enter dimensions. Use Move, Rotate, or Resize handles for visual changes. Round shapes resize evenly across their diameter.
  3. Copy a piece. Click Copy, then Paste as many times as needed. Ctrl/Cmd+C and Ctrl/Cmd+V also work outside text fields. Copies keep dimensions, angles, and material settings; each pasted piece moves independently.
  4. Hollow and pattern. Enable Hollow and choose shell thickness. On spheres, choose round or hexagonal through holes, or shallow dimples. Web spacing sets material between openings. Inspect inside shows a preview cutaway; exports keep the whole model. Try Start with → Perforated ball.
  5. Place and attach. Choose a target and side. With Move together enabled, moving or rotating the parent carries attached pieces. Targets exclude the selected piece and its children. Resizing a parent does not reposition its children; check their fit again.
  6. Make an opening. Add a cylinder or block, position it through a solid, and select Make opening. The red piece removes material from all visible solids it intersects. It remains editable.
  7. Check Print result. Overlapping solids are fused and openings are cut. Separate solids are reported and can be intentional separate pieces.
  8. Save and print. Save project keeps editable pieces in a .printstudio.json file. Export STL or 3MF, open it in your slicer, and choose orientation, filament, and temporary supports there.

This version builds from its included shapes. It does not import STL/3MF meshes, add text, predict strength, or choose slicer supports. Curved corners on blocks and trays run along their vertical edges. All dimensions use millimeters.
